### Project description:
<br>
Financial investment has become increasingly accessible to individual retail investors
through online brokerages, investment platforms, and financial information services.
These investors have access to market transactions, price movements, and other
market data with ease, but most of them do not have enough technical knowledge to
analyse and make use of the information. Although novice investors may know which
financial assets they hold, they often lack the knowledge to judge whether their
portfolio is diversified, overly concentrated, aligned with their investment goals, or
performing better or worse than market benchmarks.
</br>
<br>
This project addresses this problem by designing and implementing a financial
portfolio assistant web application for beginner and intermediate retail investors. The
system is intended to help users record simulated investment activities, derive portfolio
holdings, analyse allocation, compare performance against market benchmarks,
calculate portfolio optimisation scenarios, and receive beginner-friendly explanations
of the results. The project is designed as a decision support tool that helps users better
understand portfolio information and the implications of calculated analysis results.
However, it does not execute real trades.
</br>
